A Letter For You

Dear Samantha,

I wanted today to be about you not because you need a special day to be celebrated, but because I like any excuse to tell you how much you mean to me.

You have this way of making ordinary days feel like they matter. doing nothing in particular somehow it's better because you're the one next to me. I don't take that for granted.

Thank you for your patience, your laugh that I can pick out of any room, and the way you show up for the people you love without being asked. I hope today you feel exactly as cherished as you make me feel every day.

Here's to us, and to many more ordinary days that don't feel ordinary at all.

Yours, always

Our Story So Far

A Little Timeline

Chapter One
The Day We Met
I remember the exact moment I saw you it was like everything else in the world just disappeared. The noise, the people around us, all of it faded, and somehow you were the only thing that felt real. From that first moment, I was so interested in getting to know you not just the surface things, but who you actually were underneath it all. And honestly, I still am. That curiosity never left. There's always something new to learn about you, some small thing that makes me fall for you a little more. That's what I love most about this every day with you feels like a new adventure. Nothing about it feels routine, even on the ordinary days. You make ordinary feel like something worth paying attention to.
Chapter Two
The First Date
I still remember every detail of our first date the way you laughed at my jokes like they were the funniest thing you'd ever heard, the way you looked at me when you thought I wasn't watching, like you already knew something I hadn't figured out yet. I was so nervous that night. In my head, I kept thinking you'd take one look at how shy I was and change your mind. I was bracing myself for it. But you didn't. You stayed. You made me feel like being quiet and a little awkward was okay, like I didn't have to perform to be worth your time. That's the moment I trace it all back to. That's why I love you the way I do not because everything was perfect, but because you saw me at my most unsure and chose to stay anyway.
Chapter Three
Making It Official
I remember the exact moment I knew I wanted to spend my life with you. It wasn't loud or dramatic it just hit me, clear and certain, like something I'd always known but was only just realizing. Asking you to be my girlfriend is still one of the best things that ever happened to me. I was nervous walking up to you, half-convinced I'd trip over my own words. But the second you said yes, everything settled. Like a piece of my life finally clicked into place. Looking back now, I don't think I fully understood what I was asking for that day. I just knew I didn't want to imagine my life without you in it. And somehow, every day since has only made me more sure of that.
Today
Still Choosing You
I remember where we are now not just the moment, but everything it took to get here. The late nights, the small arguments that taught us how to actually talk to each other, the quiet, ordinary days that somehow became my favorite ones. And when I think about what's ahead, I don't feel nervous about it. I feel excited. I'm looking forward to more mornings like this, more inside jokes only we understand, more years of figuring life out side by side with you. I love you. Not just for who you are now, but for who we're becoming together.
